隐私政策

简账 SimpleLedger · 生效日期 2026 年 11 月 25 日

简账是一款只在本机记账的应用。我们不收集、不上传、不出售你的任何数据。

我们收集哪些数据

不收集。你记录的支出、分类、车辆、里程和油量都只保存在你的设备上。App 不需要注册账号，不连接任何服务器，也不包含广告或第三方统计 SDK。

数据存在哪里

数据保存在 App 在你设备上的私有存储中。删除 App 会同时删除这些数据。

导出与备份

当你主动使用“导出 Excel”或“备份数据”时，App 会生成文件并调用系统分享菜单。文件发送到哪里（例如“文件”App、邮件或其他应用）完全由你选择，我们无法访问这些文件。

面容 ID / 触控 ID

如果你开启 App 锁，解锁验证由系统完成，App 只得到“成功或失败”的结果，不会接触你的生物特征信息。

儿童隐私

App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

政策变更与联系

如本政策有变动，会在本页面更新并注明生效日期。如有疑问，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们。

Privacy Policy

SimpleLedger · Effective November 25, 2026

SimpleLedger is an expense tracker that works entirely on your device. We do not collect, upload or sell any of your data.

Data we collect

None. Your expenses, categories, vehicles, odometer readings and fuel amounts stay on your device. The app needs no account, connects to no server, and contains no ads or third-party analytics.

Where your data is stored

In the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Exports and backups

When you choose “Export Excel” or “Back Up Data”, the app creates a file and opens the system share sheet. You decide where the file goes (for example Files, Mail or another app); we have no access to it.

Face ID / Touch ID

If you turn on App Lock, the system performs the check and the app only learns whether it succeeded. The app never accesses your biometric data.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: GitHub Issues.
