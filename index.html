简账 · 技术支持

简账是一款简单、只记支出的个人记账 App：快速记一笔，按周、月、年查看统计，记录用车费用和油耗，导出 Excel。所有数据只保存在你的手机上。

常见问题

换手机后数据怎么迁移？
在“设置 → 备份数据”导出备份文件，在新手机上用“从备份恢复”导入即可。

油耗是怎么算的？
按“加满到加满”计算：两次加满之间加入的油量 ÷ 行驶里程。需要至少两次加满并填写里程和升数。

为什么没有云同步？
为了保护隐私，简账不连接任何服务器。可以用备份文件在设备之间转移数据。

SimpleLedger · Support

SimpleLedger is a simple expense tracker: add an expense in seconds, see weekly, monthly and yearly stats, track car costs and fuel economy, and export to Excel. All data stays on your phone.

FAQ

How do I move my data to a new phone?
Use Settings → Back Up Data, then Restore from Backup on the new phone.

How is fuel economy calculated?
Full-to-full: litres added between two full fill-ups ÷ distance driven. It needs at least two full fill-ups with odometer and litres.

Why no cloud sync?
For privacy, SimpleLedger never connects to a server. Use a backup file to move data between devices.
